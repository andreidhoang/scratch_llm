The invariant — every stored p = exp(s − m_ref), and O and l always share the same m_ref, so O/l equals true softmax·V no matter when you rescale. Rescaling by c = exp(m_old − m_new) just re-bases both.

Why FA4 cares — in the real kernel O is a whole tile row: each skipped rescale saves a vector multiply per row. Blackwell's tensor cores got so fast that these softmax-side ops became the bottleneck — FA4 only re-bases when the max jump exceeds τ, cutting rescales ~10×. Cost: intermediates grow up to e^τ, pressuring fp16/bf16 range — push τ to 10 and watch the bar.
